Spans: the watch cut into 300 s spans from the burst's end (1080 s). - **levels** (`c2.levels`, FILE(analysis/c2.py:85); thresholds of decisions.md 2026-11-01, FILE(docs/decisions.md:285)): in the end window, mean KV ≥ 0.80, mean N ≥ 2 × its baseline mean, maximum N < 256. The function names which failed. In this setup the cap cannot be reached (the pool holds at most about 42 requests at the smallest prompt, DERIVED by rule F, same file), and N ≥ 2 × baseline can fail only if the baseline N is above about 12 to 15 (DERIVED by rule F round 2: by tokens at the mean prompt, N is 24.2 at KV 0.80 and 28.7 at 0.95).
- **A gauge value needs coverage** (`c2.gauge`, FILE(analysis/c2.py:48)): scrapes within 10 s of each end of its window and no gap above 10 s (`load._covered`); otherwise the value is missing.
- **The exit** (`c2.last_burst_end`, FILE(analysis/c2.py:101)): the moment the last burst request ended. Missing if a burst request never ended, ended with an error status, or the run has no burst rows.
- **links** (`c2.links`, FILE(analysis/c2.py:111)), over one span:
  1. reuse of later turns (cached tokens over the previous prompt, by first token; at least 20 later turns, else missing) ≤ baseline − 0.06 (the verdict's `stuck_hit_drop`, 0.06 in every config). At full reuse this reuse is 1.0 (DERIVED from the session shape: a later turn's prefix is its previous prompt); the counter hit rate in the same warm state is about 0.84 (FILE(docs/decisions.md:3747)).
  2. session uncached prefill (prompt tokens not served from the cache, session requests only, per second, by first token) ≥ 2 × baseline;
  3. mean N ≥ 2 × baseline;
  4. mean KV ≥ 0.80.
  The function names the links that fail. Any missing value, the baseline's included, makes the span's result missing, never a failure. The uncached rate raises if an ok session request has no first-token time or prompt length; a request without `cached_tokens` counts as a full miss, and a request with a first token that never finished is not counted (both are counted and reported, `c2.row_counts`).
- **sustain** (`c2.sustain`, FILE(analysis/c2.py:125)): a span is judged when it starts at or after the exit. Every span of the watch is reported with that flag (the JSON prints it as the string "True" or "False"); the unjudged span that holds the exit mixes time before and after it.
- **What a link's threshold lets through.** A full recovery of up to about 65 to 70 s inside one span fails no link (the first failure comes by KV); the reuse link fails only at about 285 of 300 s with the cache back (DERIVED by rule F round 2 on a baseline of 1.0, E1 and E2). So `loop` excludes a span with the cache back for more than about 240 of its 300 s, or with a full recovery of more than about 70 s, at 300 s resolution.
- **Why 2 × for the uncached rate (fixed before any run is read for it):** DERIVED from the L1 schedule. Warm, a session of 8 turns prefills 2176 + 7 × 256 = 3968 tokens; cold, 24,576. At equal request rates the cold rate is 6.2 × the warm one (DERIVED: 24,576 / 3968). At the cold completion rates on record (1.73 to 1.81 req/s, RUN(20261110-182920_burst_l1pl350cl_r2.0_s2, gate.reg17_gate), RUN(20261110-063647_burst_l1pl350cl_r2.2_s2, gate.reg14_gate)) against 2.0 to 2.4 req/s offered it is about 4.5 to 5.6 × (DERIVED). ### Labels (`c2.condition4`, FILE(analysis/c2.py:172))
In this order:
1. The levels cannot be read (a gauge does not cover the end window or the baseline): `undefined`.
2. The levels fail: `not_memory_limited`, whatever `sustain` is.
3. The levels hold and there are fewer than two judged spans (or no exit): `undefined`. One judged span that fails is still `undefined`.
4. The levels hold and a judged span fails a link: `not_sustained`, even if another judged span has a missing value.
5. The levels hold, no judged span fails, and one has a missing value: `undefined`.
6. Otherwise: `loop`.
