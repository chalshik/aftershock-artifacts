The function names which failed. In this setup the cap cannot be reached (the pool holds at most about 42 requests at the smallest prompt, DERIVED by rule F, same file), and N ≥ 2 × baseline can fail only if the baseline N is above about 12 to 15 (DERIVED by rule F round 2: by tokens at the mean prompt, N is 24.2 at KV 0.80 and 28.7 at 0.95).
- **A gauge value needs coverage** (`c2.gauge`, FILE(analysis/c2.py:48)): scrapes within 10 s of each end of its window and no gap above 10 s (`load._covered`); otherwise the value is missing.
- **The exit** (`c2.last_burst_end`, FILE(analysis/c2.py:101)): the moment the last burst request ended. Missing if a burst request never ended, ended with an error status, or the run has no burst rows.
- **links** (`c2.links`, FILE(analysis/c2.py:111)), over one span:
  1. reuse of later turns (cached tokens over the previous prompt, by first token; at least 20 later turns, else missing) ≤ baseline − 0.06 (the verdict's `stuck_hit_drop`, 0.06 in every config). At full reuse this reuse is 1.0 (DERIVED from the session shape: a later turn's prefix is its previous prompt); the counter hit rate in the same warm state is about 0.84 (FILE(docs/decisions.md:3747)).
  2. session uncached prefill (prompt tokens not served from the cache, session requests only, per second, by first token) ≥ 2 × baseline;
  3. mean N ≥ 2 × baseline;
  4. mean KV ≥ 0.80.
  The function names the links that fail. Any missing value, the baseline's included, makes the span's result missing, never a failure. The uncached rate raises if an ok session request has no first-token time or prompt length; a request without `cached_tokens` counts as a full miss, and a request with a first token that never finished is not counted (both are counted and reported, `c2.row_counts`).
- **sustain** (`c2.sustain`, FILE(analysis/c2.py:125)): a span is judged when it starts at or after the exit. Every span of the watch is reported with that flag (the JSON prints it as the string "True" or "False"); the unjudged span that holds the exit mixes time before and after it.
- **What a link's threshold lets through.** A full recovery of up to about 65 to 70 s inside one span fails no link (the first failure comes by KV); the reuse link fails only at about 285 of 300 s with the cache back (DERIVED by rule F round 2 on a baseline of 1.0, E1 and E2). So `loop` excludes a span with the cache back for more than about 240 of its 300 s, or with a full recovery of more than about 70 s, at 300 s resolution.

At equal request rates the cold rate is 6.2 × the warm one (DERIVED: 24,576 / 3968). At the cold completion rates on record (1.73 to 1.81 req/s, RUN(20261110-182920_burst_l1pl350cl_r2.0_s2, gate.reg17_gate), RUN(20261110-063647_burst_l1pl350cl_r2.2_s2, gate.reg14_gate)) against 2.0 to 2.4 req/s offered it is about 4.5 to 5.6 × (DERIVED). ### Labels (`c2.condition4`, FILE(analysis/c2.py:172))
In this order:
1. The levels cannot be read (a gauge does not cover the end window or the baseline): `undefined`.
2. The levels fail: `not_memory_limited`, whatever `sustain` is.
3. The levels hold and there are fewer than two judged spans (or no exit): `undefined`. One judged span that fails is still `undefined`.
4. The levels hold and a judged span fails a link: `not_sustained`, even if another judged span has a missing value.
5. The levels hold, no judged span fails, and one has a missing value: `undefined`.
6. Otherwise: `loop`.

The crossing lags differ by construction: for a step at one instant from the baseline value to the end-window value, a trailing 60 s mean crosses the level 60 × (level − before) ÷ (after − before) seconds after the step. At values on record that is about 3.6 s for the reuse (DERIVED: 60 × 0.06 / 1.0; 4.3 s on a baseline of 0.84), 11.5 to 17 s for the uncached rate (DERIVED: 60 / (6.2 − 1) to 60 / (4.5 − 1)), 15 s for N (60 × 6 / 24) and about 56 s for KV (DERIVED: 60 × (0.9 − 0.24) / (0.95 − 0.24), warm KV 0.23–0.24 from FILE(docs/decisions.md:2477) and FILE(docs/decisions.md:2478)). The grid step is in the tolerance because onsets are rounded up to the 10 s grid: without it, four signals stepping together read as ordered in 2 of 10 step phases (rule F round 2, trace t05 O). A ramp crosses with another lag; that is not modelled.
- **The eviction link read directly** (`c2.eviction_readout`): the engine samples 1 % of its KV blocks and, when a sampled block is taken for a new allocation, records the time since its last access (`vllm:kv_block_idle_before_evict_seconds`; SOURCE(vLLM 0.32.0 vllm/v1/core/kv_cache_metrics.py:KVCacheMetricsCollector.on_block_evicted); SOURCE(vLLM 0.32.0 vllm/v1/core/block_pool.py:_maybe_evict_cached_block)). ### Alternatives to state with any result
1. **Overload, not a loop.** At λ = 2.4 the closed-loop warm capacity is reached (FILE(docs/claims.md:45)), and REG-12's seed-5 control fell into the same cold, full, backlogged state with no burst (FILE(docs/decisions.md:2478)). 2. **The label follows from the selection.** The verdict `stuck` already requires a low hit rate at the end; the levels then nearly follow. What is not selected on is the spans before the end window.
3. **The preemption cycle of Ao et al.** A full pool with a large batch is also what their limit cycle shows. **This registration does not separate it.** The registered comparison (preemptions per minute against the same seed's control, decisions.md 2026-11-01) has no code and no registration (FILE(docs/definitions.md:102)).
4. **A schedule lull.** A span planned below the cold rate can shrink the batch and KV with the cache still lost; each span's planned rate is reported, and `not_sustained` is worded by the link that failed (line e). The planned rate is by `t_planned`, the schedule time; in closed loop a lull reaches the server later, by the backlog's delay.
5. **More spans, more chances.** A run with 14 judged spans is likelier to be `not_sustained` than one with 4; the number of judged spans is reported per run and no rate is compared across watch lengths.
6. **The deepest backlogs drop out.** The longest burst tails leave the fewest judged spans, so the most stuck runs are the likeliest to be `undefined`; the spans that exist are reported for those runs.
7. **Late first tokens, for any onset result.** Session signals are read by first token, and the uncached rate is session work per second. While the burst shares the server, session turns are served late or not at all and the uncached rate is diluted, so both cross late for reasons of measurement (FILE(docs/drafts/reviews-2026-11-13/rulef-reg22-draft5-r3.md): t03b F; t03 C and C2, where a cache lost at 900 s and one lost at 1000 s give the same onsets and reading). A `reversed` pair says nothing about when the cache was lost unless every trailing 60 s window through the burst held at least 20 later turns, which this registration does not compute. The reuse is a ratio and is not diluted while such a window holds 20 turns; only the pairs with the uncached rate carry the dilution.
