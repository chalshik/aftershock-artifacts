- **Onset reading** (`c2.onset_reading`). The crossing lags differ by construction: for a step at one instant from the baseline value to the end-window value, a trailing 60 s mean crosses the level 60 × (level − before) ÷ (after − before) seconds after the step. At values on record that is about 3.6 s for the reuse (DERIVED: 60 × 0.06 / 1.0; 4.3 s on a baseline of 0.84), 11.5 to 17 s for the uncached rate (DERIVED: 60 / (6.2 − 1) to 60 / (4.5 − 1)), 15 s for N (60 × 6 / 24) and about 56 s for KV (DERIVED: 60 × (0.9 − 0.24) / (0.95 − 0.24), warm KV 0.23–0.24 from FILE(docs/decisions.md:2477) and FILE(docs/decisions.md:2478)). The code takes each run's own baseline and end-window values, so each onset minus its own lag is the step time it implies. For each pair in the plan's order (reuse, uncached rate, N, KV), `plan` if the later signal's implied step is more than one grid step (10 s) after the earlier one's, `reversed` if more than 10 s before, `together` otherwise. The run's reading: `incomplete` if a signal has no onset or its end value does not pass its level; `differs` if any pair is `reversed`; `plan_order_beyond_lags` if every pair is `plan`; else `not_distinguishable_from_simultaneous`. The grid step is in the tolerance because onsets are rounded up to the 10 s grid: without it, four signals stepping together read as ordered in 2 of 10 step phases (rule F round 2, trace t05 O). A ramp crosses with another lag; that is not modelled.
- **The eviction link read directly** (`c2.eviction_readout`): the engine samples 1 % of its KV blocks and, when a sampled block is taken for a new allocation, records the time since its last access (`vllm:kv_block_idle_before_evict_seconds`; SOURCE(vLLM 0.32.0 vllm/v1/core/kv_cache_metrics.py:KVCacheMetricsCollector.on_block_evicted); SOURCE(vLLM 0.32.0 vllm/v1/core/block_pool.py:_maybe_evict_cached_block)). For the baseline and for the stretch after the exit (after the burst's end where there is no exit): what the histogram gained, the bucket holding the median with its lower and upper edge, the shares at or below 5 s and 30 s, and the median time between the first tokens of a session's consecutive turns (`c2.turn_spacing`). A bucket that is lower than at the scrape before, at any scrape of the window, flags the window `reset`, and it is not read (N8). Where the turn spacing falls inside the median's bucket, the two are reported as not separable.
  - **Prediction (DERIVED in `agent/theory/k1_held_prefix.md`; BELIEF as a forecast):** warm, an idle prefix is overwritten about 64 s after its release, so the median falls in the (60, 120] s bucket, far above a turn spacing of about 7 s; in the cold state about 1 s after its release, so the median is the time a request holds its blocks plus that, in the (10, 20] or (20, 30] s bucket, and below the turn spacing there.
  - **If it is wrong:** a cold-state median above the turn spacing would mean prefixes outlive the gap and are still not reused, which the loop does not explain; a warm median below the turn spacing would contradict the warm counter hit rate of about 0.84 (FILE(docs/decisions.md:3747)). Either is reported as against the loop's eviction link.
